Controls & connection help

Keyboard: W/S to move, A/D to strafe, arrows or mouse to turn. Click or F to zorch, E or Space to use doors, Shift to run, 1–7 to select a zorcher, Tab for the map. Use or zorch to respawn. Escape opens the original menu.

On phones, turn sideways and use the large touch buttons. Keep both game tabs visible. On Android, allow Nearby devices / Local network for your browser and accept any local-network prompt.

Some networks need a TURN relay. Optional connection settings stay in this tab.
